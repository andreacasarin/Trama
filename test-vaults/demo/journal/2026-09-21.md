# 2026-09-21 — A notebook worth demonstrating

Today I sketched the story I want the Trama demo to tell. Start with a real note, not a settings screen. Find an idea with Lente, follow a neighboring thought with Filo, then ask Voce to connect the two.

The collection should look like something a developer would keep while building a local-first assistant. Short technical notes can explain the mechanisms; daily entries can record the uncertainty and decisions around them. Neither needs to sound like a product brochure.

My first example will be [[Embedding cache reuse]]. It has a clear user benefit and connects to several deeper questions. The follow-up query is “How do we avoid repeating work on unchanged notes?”

Next: prepare the exact-word search example in [[Lexical search remembers exact words]], then choose a question that needs more than one source. Keep screenshots focused on the interaction rather than filling every panel.
