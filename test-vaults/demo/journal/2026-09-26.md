# 2026-09-26 — Honest performance claims

I wrote down a rule for any future performance screenshot: include the measurement boundary. A number without its workload and environment is easy to misread.

Our benchmark uses deterministic provider fixtures. That is useful for comparing the index lifecycle, but it does not measure a real model's inference latency or relevance. In-memory publication also excludes filesystem publication costs.

[[Structured timing events]] should help distinguish preparation, transport, SQL, and provider work. I want those same events available during real usage, because a slow experience in a live notebook may not resemble the fixed benchmark at all.

No speed claims in the demo notes yet. The product story is that the workflow is understandable and grounded. When we have representative evidence, the numbers can support that story. Until then, [[Retrieval evaluation]] and [[Publication is a coherent snapshot]] explain the boundaries better than a chart with a dramatic headline.
