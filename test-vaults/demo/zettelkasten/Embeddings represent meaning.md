# Embeddings represent meaning

An embedding turns a piece of text into a list of numbers. Texts with related meanings can end up near each other even when they share few words. That makes embeddings useful for finding a forgotten idea rather than a remembered phrase.

The representation is model-dependent. A vector is not a universal address for an idea, and it does not prove that two passages say the same thing. Short, ambiguous notes can be especially difficult to interpret without context.

I think of semantic retrieval as a candidate generator. It offers plausible passages; the reader still checks them. This is why results should keep their note titles and readable excerpts.
