# Voce grounds a conversation

Voce turns retrieval into a conversation about the notebook. Instead of listing passages alone, it can help compare ideas, summarize a decision, or identify an unresolved question.

The conversation should stay grounded in sources. A fluent answer is not evidence that the right notes were retrieved, and a retrieved passage is not permission to invent details around it. Keeping source references visible lets the reader inspect what supports the response.

A useful demo question is: “Why do we keep lexical search alongside embeddings?” This notebook contains several complementary answers. The assistant should connect them and leave a clear route back to the notes.
