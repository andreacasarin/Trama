# Citations are an interface

A source reference is not decoration attached to an answer. It is an action the reader can take to inspect the underlying note.

Good references preserve a recognizable title and lead to useful context. If an assistant quotes a passage but the reader cannot find it again, the conversation has created a new navigation problem instead of solving one.

This matters in screenshots too. A marketing image should show a question, a readable answer, and visible sources rather than a wall of uncheckable prose. The product story is grounded assistance, not omniscience. I want people to see that their own notes remain the authority.
